How this game teaches cross-sections

What am I actually doing?

Every swipe is a plane through the spirit. The flat face it leaves behind is a cross-section, and its shape depends only on the angle you chose.

Reading the swipe

The blade lies along the line you draw. Across the spirit → flat face → circle or square. Up and down → upright face → rectangle or triangle. Diagonal → ellipse, trapezium or hexagon.

For the teacher

17 vows across prisms, pyramids, cylinder, cone and sphere. The blade is continuous, and a swipe within 7° of vertical on a pyramid or cone clicks onto its apex: a point is too fine to aim at. Every vow is proven winnable by dev/check_vows.js.
