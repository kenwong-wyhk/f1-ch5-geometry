A
Acute angle 銳角
An angle greater than 0° and less than 90°.
Acute-angled triangle 銳角三角形
A triangle in which all three interior angles are acute.
Adjacent angles 鄰角
Two angles that share a common vertex and a common arm, and do not overlap. Adjacent angles on a straight line add up to 180°.
Apex 錐頂
The single top point of a pyramid or a cone, where the lateral faces (or the curved surface) meet.
Arc 弧
A part of the circumference of a circle. The shorter arc is the minor arc; the longer one is the major arc.
Area 面積
The amount of surface a 2-D figure covers, measured in square units (cm²).
B
Base 底 / 底面
In a prism, one of the two identical parallel end faces. In a pyramid or cone, the single face on which it stands.
C
Centre 圓心
The fixed point inside a circle, from which every point on the circle is the same distance. Usually named O.
Chord 弦
A line segment joining any two points on a circle. A diameter is a chord that passes through the centre.
Circle 圓
The set of all points in a plane that are a fixed distance from a fixed point (the centre).
Circumference 圓周
The complete boundary, or perimeter, of a circle.
Complementary angles 餘角
Two angles whose sum is 90°. Each is the complement of the other.
Concave polygon 凹多邊形
A polygon with at least one interior angle greater than 180°: it has a “dent”.
Cone 圓錐
A solid with one circular base, one apex and one curved surface. It is not a pyramid.
Construction 作圖
A drawing made accurately with instruments (ruler, set square, protractor, compasses) rather than by estimating.
Convex polygon 凸多邊形
A polygon in which every interior angle is less than 180°.
Cross-section 截面
The plane figure exposed when a solid is cut by a plane.
Cube 正方體
A solid with 6 identical square faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.
Cuboid 長方體
A solid with 6 rectangular faces. Also called a rectangular prism.
Curve 曲線
A line that is not straight, e.g. an arc of a circle.
Cylinder 圓柱
A solid with two identical circular ends joined by a curved surface. It has uniform cross-sections.
D
Decagon 十邊形
A polygon with 10 sides.
Degree 度
The unit for measuring angles, written °. A full turn is 360°.
Diagonal 對角線
A line segment joining two non-adjacent vertices of a polygon. An n-sided polygon has n(n − 3) ÷ 2 diagonals.
Diameter 直徑
A chord that passes through the centre of a circle; the longest chord. Diameter = 2 × radius.
E
Edge 稜
The line segment where two faces of a solid meet.
End face 端面
One of the two identical parallel faces of a prism; it gives the prism its name.
Equiangular polygon 等角多邊形
A polygon whose interior angles are all equal (e.g. a rectangle). Not necessarily regular.
Equilateral polygon 等邊多邊形
A polygon whose sides are all equal (e.g. a rhombus). Not necessarily regular.
Equilateral triangle 等邊三角形
A triangle with three equal sides; each interior angle is 60°.
F
Face 面
A flat surface of a solid. A cube has 6 faces.
H
Hemisphere 半球
Half of a sphere, cut by a plane through its centre.
Heptagon 七邊形
A polygon with 7 sides.
Hexagon 六邊形
A polygon with 6 sides.
Hypotenuse 斜邊
The side opposite the right angle in a right-angled triangle; the longest side.
I
Interior angle 內角
An angle inside a polygon at one of its vertices.
Isosceles triangle 等腰三角形
A triangle with two equal sides; the two angles at the base are also equal.
L
Lateral face 側面
A side face of a prism or pyramid: rectangles in a prism, triangles in a pyramid.
Line 直線
A straight path with no endpoints and no thickness. It has no length.
Line segment 線段
A part of a line with two endpoints and a fixed length.
N
Nonagon 九邊形
A polygon with 9 sides.
Non-polyhedron 非多面體
A solid with at least one curved surface, e.g. a cylinder, cone or sphere.
O
Obtuse angle 鈍角
An angle greater than 90° and less than 180°.
Obtuse-angled triangle 鈍角三角形
A triangle with one obtuse interior angle.
Octagon 八邊形
A polygon with 8 sides.
P
Parallel lines 平行線
Lines in the same plane that never meet, however far they are extended. Written AB // CD.
Pentagon 五邊形
A polygon with 5 sides.
Perpendicular lines 垂直線
Lines that meet at a right angle. Written AB ⊥ CD.
Plane 平面
A flat surface with no thickness that extends in all directions. Also called a surface.
Point 點
An exact position with no size. Named with a capital letter.
Polygon 多邊形
A closed plane figure bounded by three or more straight line segments that do not cross.
Polyhedron 多面體
A solid enclosed by polygons only: no curved surfaces.
Prism 稜柱
A solid with two identical parallel end faces and rectangular lateral faces. It has uniform cross-sections.
Protractor 量角器
An instrument for measuring and drawing angles in degrees.
Pyramid 稜錐
A solid with one polygonal base and triangular lateral faces meeting at an apex.
Q
Quadrilateral 四邊形
A polygon with 4 sides.
R
Radius 半徑
A segment from the centre of a circle to a point on the circle; also its length. Plural: radii.
Reflex angle 反角
An angle greater than 180° and less than 360°.
Regular polygon 正多邊形
A polygon with all sides equal and all interior angles equal.
Rhombus 菱形
A quadrilateral with four equal sides. It is equilateral but not necessarily regular.
Right angle 直角
An angle of exactly 90°, marked with a small square.
Right-angled triangle 直角三角形
A triangle with one right angle. The side opposite it is the hypotenuse.
Round angle 周角
An angle of exactly 360°: one complete turn.
S
Scalene triangle 不等邊三角形
A triangle with all three sides of different lengths.
Sector 扇形
The region of a circle enclosed by two radii and an arc.
Segment (of a circle) 弓形
The region of a circle enclosed by a chord and an arc.
Semicircle 半圓
Half of a circle, cut off by a diameter.
Set square 三角尺
A drawing instrument in the shape of a right-angled triangle, used with a ruler for parallel and perpendicular lines.
Solid 立體
A 3-D figure with faces, edges and vertices (or curved surfaces).
Sphere 球
A solid with every point the same distance from its centre; its whole surface is curved and every cross-section is a circle.
Square 正方形
A quadrilateral with four equal sides and four right angles: a regular polygon.
Straight angle 平角
An angle of exactly 180°: the two arms form a straight line.
Supplementary angles 補角
Two angles whose sum is 180°. Each is the supplement of the other.
Surface 面
A flat 2-D extent with no thickness; a plane.
T
Tetrahedron 四面體
A solid with 4 triangular faces: a triangular pyramid. It has 4 vertices and 6 edges.
Triangle 三角形
A polygon with 3 sides, 3 vertices and 3 interior angles. The angle sum is 180°.
U
Uniform cross-section 均勻橫截面
A property of a solid whose cross-sections, cut parallel to the end faces, all have the same shape and size as the end faces.
V
Vertex 頂點
A corner where two sides or two edges meet. Plural: vertices.
Vertical 垂直方向
At right angles to the horizontal: straight up and down.
