Circles

圓 — 圓心、半徑、直徑、弦、弧、扇形、弓形

Learning objectives

  1. Understand the concept of a circle.
  2. Name the parts of a circle correctly: centre, radius, diameter, chord, arc, sector, segment.
  3. Use the relationship diameter = 2 × radius.
  4. Avoid mixing up a chord, a diameter, a sector and a segment.

1What is a circle? 甚麼是圓

A circle is the set of all points that are the same distance from a fixed point. That fixed point is the centre.

Definition

A circle is a plane figure made of all the points that are a fixed distance from a fixed point called the centre.

The distance from the centre to any point on the circle is the same everywhere — that is what makes it a circle and not an oval.

Real life: a wheel, a clock face, a coin, the rim of a cup, a pizza.

3The one relationship you must know 直徑與半徑

Diameter and radius

diameter = 2 × radius

radius = diameter ÷ 2

A diameter is made of two radii in a straight line, so it is always exactly twice as long as a radius.

Three facts worth remembering

  • All radii of the same circle are equal.
  • The diameter is the longest chord of a circle.
  • A chord does not have to pass through the centre — only a diameter does.
Notation

There is no special symbol for a circle in this chapter — write “the circle with centre O”. An arc can be written with a curved mark above the letters, e.g. arc AB. Always give the centre when naming a circle, because two circles can share the same two points.

4Worked examples 例題

Example 1Radius and diameter

A circle with centre O has radius 7.5 cm.

(a) Find its diameter.    (b) A chord of the circle is 16 cm long. Is that possible?

(a) diameter = 2 × 7.5 cm = 15 cm

(b) No. The longest possible chord is the diameter, 15 cm. A 16 cm chord would not fit inside the circle.

Example 2Name that part

In the circle with centre O, A and B are points on the circle and AB passes through O. C and D are also on the circle, and CD does not pass through O.

(a) What is AB called?    (b) What is CD called?
(c) What is OA called?    (d) Which is longer, AB or CD?

(a) A diameter — a chord passing through the centre.

(b) A chord (not a diameter, because it does not pass through O).

(c) A radius.

(d) AB is longer. The diameter is the longest chord.

Example 3True or false

Decide whether each statement must be correct.

(a) A line segment joining any two points on a circle is a diameter.
(b) All radii of a circle are equal.
(c) A sector is bounded by a chord and an arc.

(a) False. It is a chord. It is only a diameter if it passes through the centre.

(b) True. That is the defining property of a circle.

(c) False. That describes a segment. A sector is bounded by two radii and an arc.

5Let's check 課堂練習

Check yourself
  1. Fill in the blanks: a diameter is the longest ______ of a circle.
  2. A circle has diameter 24 cm. What is its radius?
  3. Two circles have radii 3 cm and 6 cm. What are their diameters?
  4. Name the region bounded by two radii and an arc.
  5. Name the region bounded by a chord and an arc.
  6. Can a chord be longer than a diameter? Explain.
  7. How many diameters does a circle have?
  1. … the longest chord.
  2. 24 ÷ 2 = 12 cm
  3. 6 cm and 12 cm
  4. A sector (扇形).
  5. A segment (弓形).
  6. No. The diameter passes through the centre, so it is the longest possible chord.
  7. Infinitely many — every line through the centre cuts the circle into a diameter.

!Common mistakes 常見錯誤

✗ Wrong“Any segment joining two points on a circle is a diameter.”
✓ RightIt is a chord; a diameter must pass through the centre
This exact statement is a standard multiple-choice trap. Read it carefully — the word “diameter” hides the missing condition.
✗ WrongMixing up sector and segment
✓ RightSector = 2 radii + arc; Segment = 1 chord + arc
A sector reaches the centre (like a pizza slice); a segment does not.
✗ Wrongradius = 2 × diameter
✓ Rightdiameter = 2 × radius
The diameter goes all the way across, so it is the bigger one.
✗ WrongCalling the circumference “the circle”
✓ RightThe circumference is the boundary; the circle includes everything inside
In everyday speech they are the same; in geometry they are not.
✗ Wrong“The arc is a straight line from A to B.”
✓ RightAn arc is curved; the straight segment AB is a chord
Two different objects between the same two points: one curved (arc), one straight (chord).
One-line summary

Everything starts at the centre. Centre → circle = radius; through the centre to the far side = diameter (= 2r); any two points = chord; two radii + arc = sector; chord + arc = segment.
