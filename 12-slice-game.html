What is a cross-section?

The flat face you see after a plane cuts through a solid. Its shape depends on where the plane goes and which way it is tilted — that is the whole game.

Two rules that solve half the levels

Parallel to the base → same shape as the base. Straight through the apex of a pyramid or cone → a triangle.
