1What is a polygon? 甚麼是多邊形

A polygon is a closed plane figure made of straight line segments that do not cross each other. Triangles and quadrilaterals are polygons; a circle is not.

The four conditions

  1. It is flat (a plane figure).
  2. It is closed: no gaps, no loose ends.
  3. Every side is a straight line segment: no curves.
  4. The sides do not cross each other.
Vocabulary of every polygon
  • Vertex (頂點, plural vertices): a corner where two sides meet.
  • Side (邊): one of the straight segments forming the boundary.
  • Interior angle (內角): the angle inside the polygon at a vertex.
  • Diagonal (對角線): a segment joining two non-adjacent vertices.

Word parts

3Convex and concave 凸多邊形與凹多邊形

Convex polygon 凸多邊形

All interior angles are less than 180°.
It “bulges outwards”: no dent.

Concave polygon 凹多邊形

At least one interior angle is greater than 180°.
It has a “dent”.

How to test it in two seconds

Put your pencil on a vertex and trace the boundary. If you have to turn backwards at some corner (the inside angle opens past a straight line), the polygon is concave. If every corner turns the same way, it is convex.

4Equilateral, equiangular, regular 等邊、等角、正多邊形

Three words that look alike but mean different things. The exam loves the difference.

Equilateral polygon 等邊多邊形

All sides are equal (matching tick marks).
Angles may differ → not necessarily regular.

Equiangular polygon 等角多邊形

All interior angles are equal.
Sides may differ → not necessarily regular.

Regular polygon 正多邊形

All sides and all interior angles are equal: ticks and right angles.
Regular = equilateral + equiangular.

The trap

“All sides are equal, therefore it is a regular polygon.”: False. A rhombus has four equal sides but its angles are not all equal, so it is equilateral but not regular. You need both conditions.

5Diagonals 對角線

A diagonal joins two vertices that are not next to each other. Sides do not count as diagonals.

Counting diagonals

From one vertex of an n-sided polygon you can draw n − 3 diagonals: you cannot join a vertex to itself, nor to its two neighbours.

Each diagonal is counted twice (once from each end), so:

number of diagonals = n(n − 3) ÷ 2

Extension · interior angle sum of a polygon

Cut the polygon into triangles by drawing diagonals from one vertex. An n-sided polygon splits into n − 2 triangles, and each triangle contributes 180°:

sum of interior angles = (n − 2) × 180°

For a regular polygon each angle is (n − 2) × 180° ÷ n. This formula is not required in this chapter: you will study it formally later. The two lines above are here so the numbers in the explorer make sense.

Example 2How many diagonals?

How many diagonals are there in a regular pentagon?

  1. A pentagon has n = 5 sides.
  2. From one vertex: n − 3 = 2 diagonals.
  3. There are 5 vertices, giving 5 × 2 = 10 “ends” of diagonals.
  4. Each diagonal has 2 ends, so divide by 2: 10 ÷ 2 = 5.

A regular pentagon has 5 diagonals.

8Let’s check 課堂練習

Check yourself
  1. Name the polygon with 7 sides.
  2. True or false: every equilateral polygon is regular. Explain.
  3. True or false: a square is a regular polygon.
  4. How many diagonals does an octagon have?
  5. Can a regular polygon be concave? Why or why not?
  6. A triangle has 3 sides. How many diagonals does it have? Does the formula still work?
  1. A heptagon.
  2. False. A rhombus is equilateral but its angles are not all equal.
  3. True: four equal sides and four equal (90°) angles.
  4. 8(8 − 3) ÷ 2 = 20.
  5. No. In a regular polygon every interior angle is equal; if one had to be greater than 180°, all of them would be, and the sides could not close up. All regular polygons are convex.
  6. Zero. The formula gives 3(3 − 3) ÷ 2 = 0: it still works.

!Common mistakes 常見錯誤

✗ Wrong“All sides equal → regular polygon”
✓ RightRegular needs equal sides and equal angles
A rhombus is the classic counter-example.
✗ Wrong“A rectangle is a regular polygon”
✓ RightA rectangle is equiangular but not equilateral
Only a square among rectangles is regular.
✗ WrongCounting sides as diagonals
✓ RightDiagonals join non-adjacent vertices only
A pentagon has 5 sides and 5 diagonals: do not add them together by mistake.
✗ Wrong“Concave = a shape with a hole”
✓ RightConcave = at least one interior angle > 180°
The test is the angle, not the overall look. A “dented” outline is the usual symptom.
✗ WrongCalling any 4-sided shape a “square”
✓ RightSquare = 4 equal sides + 4 right angles
Otherwise it is just a quadrilateral (or a rectangle / rhombus / parallelogram).
One-line summary

A polygon is closed, flat and made of straight sides. Convex = no angle over 180°; regular = equal sides and equal angles. Diagonals: n(n − 3) ÷ 2.
